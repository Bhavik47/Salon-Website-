(11) Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Message us — a real person replies.

Why don't you list prices online?

Because hair length, density and skin needs vary so much, a fixed number is rarely accurate. Share a photo or your requirement on WhatsApp and we'll send an exact quote in minutes — with no surprises at the counter.

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend booking so your chosen artist is ready for you, especially on weekends. Walk-ins are welcome whenever there's a free chair — call ahead and we'll tell you honestly.

Do you do bridal makeup at the venue or at home?

Yes. Our bridal team travels across Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad, and for destination weddings across India.

Which products do you use?

Only professional, salon-grade brands such as Kérastase, L'Oréal Professionnel, Olaplex, O.P.I, Dermalogica and Huda Beauty. Have sensitivities? Tell us and we'll patch-test first.

What is your cancellation policy?

Plans change — just let us know at least 4 hours before your slot and we'll happily reschedule. Bridal bookings have their own terms, shared with your quote.

Do you have services for men?

Absolutely — cuts, beard sculpting, colour, facials, de-tan and the full Groom's Edit, in a relaxed, no-fuss setting.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, digital gift cards in any value, delivered on WhatsApp with a personal note. A lovely last-minute present.

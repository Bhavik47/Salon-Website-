    (11) Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp. A real person replies.

    Why don't you list prices online?

    Hair length, density and skin needs vary so much that a fixed number is rarely accurate. Share your requirement and we'll send an exact quote in minutes, with no surprises at the counter.

    Do I need an appointment?

    We recommend booking so your chosen artist is ready for you, especially on weekends. Walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is free.

    Do you do bridal makeup at the venue or at home?

    Yes. Our bridal team travels across Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad, and to destination weddings across India.

    Which products do you use?

    Only professional, salon-grade brands such as Kérastase, L'Oréal Professionnel, Olaplex, O.P.I and Dermalogica. Have sensitivities? We'll patch-test first.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    Let us know at least 4 hours before your slot and we'll happily reschedule. Bridal bookings have their own terms, shared with your quote.

    Do you offer gift cards?

    Yes, digital gift cards in any value, delivered on WhatsApp with a personal note.
